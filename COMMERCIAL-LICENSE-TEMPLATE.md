Unless expressly authorized in the signed order or required by applicable law, the licensee may not redistribute, resell, sublicense, publicly publish source code, share access outside authorized personnel, or use the deliverables to create a competing reusable template or product. Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. Customer content and branding remain with their owners. Third-party software, fonts, photographs, and services retain their own licenses; the licensor must identify applicable notices and may grant only rights it holds. This agreement does not revoke previously granted open-source rights.

Private source code and non-public technical or business information must be protected with reasonable care and shared only with authorized personnel subject to confidentiality obligations.
